Kia Cordless Drill

A branded power tool concept built on Kia's "Opposites United" design language.

Category Product Design
Role Industrial Designer
Tools Sketching, Digital Rendering
Timeline [Add timeline]
Year [Add year]

Overview

This project rapidly proposes a powered handheld tool for an automotive brand — referencing Kia's current styling conventions and drawing inspiration from at least two of their vehicles.

Kia's brand identity is built on "Opposites United," using contrast inspired by nature and humanity to create a visual harmony that feels both bold and calm. The brand's "family face" is anchored by the Tiger Nose form on its grilles, reinforced by Star Map signature lighting and sharp, crisp headlamp shapes that make the front end feel precise and modern — both referenced here from the Sportage and Seltos.

Process

The drill was broken down into the parts that drive how it's actually used — battery, chuck, vent, trigger, and grip — since together they define the tool's silhouette, balance, and how compact it feels in the hand. Grip, trigger, and ventilation ideation each translated a specific Kia signature (Tiger Nose, Star Map, "Opposites United," sharp headlight shapes) into a physical form, tested across several colorways before landing on a final direction.

Outcome

The final concept translates Kia's design language directly onto the tool: a tiger-nose-shaped grip, a star map pattern across the handle, an "opposites united" trigger, and sharp headlight-inspired detailing along the body — a cordless drill that reads unmistakably as Kia.
